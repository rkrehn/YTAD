# YouTube Album Downloader (YTAD)

The software seamlessly downloads full albums from any YouTube Music playlist in the highest available MP3 format.

Simply copy and paste the playlist link from YouTube Music into YTAD. With one click, it finds the best audio sources from the related YouTube video and stitches together the full album. It's perfect for use with [Plex Music](https://www.plex.tv/plexamp/). 

### Features

* Supports artist discographies
* Supports single albums
* MP3 Downloading
* IDV3 tagging

## Tutorial
### Downloading an artist's discography

1. Open a browser and go to [https://music.youtube.com](https://music.youtube.com)
2. Find an artist and click on their artist page
3. Copy the URL
4. Paste the URL in YouTube Album Downloader at the top
5. Click "+ Artist"

### Downloading a single album

1. Open a browser and go to [https://music.youtube.com](https://music.youtube.com)
2. Find the album you want to download
3. Copy the URL
4. Paste the URL in YouTube Album Downloader at the top
5. Click "+ Album"

Press "Resume" to begin downloading music. Subdirectories will include one subdirectory for each artist, a subdirectory for each album, and finally, the mp3s will be listed by track number and song name. Tags will be automatically applied based on the artist, album, song, and song number pulled.For example: "C:\Downloads\Lord Krehn\A Haunting at Dawn\3 - The Wretching Hour.mp3"
